# Moot

**Every household deserves a parliament.**

Moot lets family members propose house rules by voice, debate them, and vote. Passed bills become laws that stay on the books, with penalties and expiry dates. The Clerk, a voice assistant, reminds the household when a law is due and rules on "is this allowed?" questions by citing the act and the date it passed.

Built for the Alexa+ track of the Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026.

Setup instructions are in progress.
